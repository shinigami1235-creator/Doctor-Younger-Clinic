Results from ACTIVE BRIGHT

  • Restores and nourishes the face for smooth, clear skin
  • Fades fine lines on the face for smooth skin and helps tighten pores

  • Reduces inflammation and redness from acne on the face

How ACTIVE BRIGHT Works

ACTIVE BRIGHT is a BRIGHT LASER that brightens the skin with high-intensity light energy similar to a camera flash, emitted in short bursts and filtered to give the right range of light frequencies, which can be varied to suit the use. The LASER delivered into the skin focuses on the problems in each skin layer, from treating abnormal pigment, the cause of dark spots and various wrinkles, without damaging the skin layers. It also helps reduce inflammation of blood vessels and the capillaries under the skin, reduces the inflammation of redness from acne, and effectively treats dull skin from sun exposure, for smoother, fairer and brighter skin. ACTIVE BRIGHT is another laser that helps make the face fairer and brighter, fades dark spots, and visibly tightens pores, increases skin elasticity and fades wrinkles, so the skin looks healthy. It also stimulates collagen and elastin under the skin, giving soft, moisturized skin.

ACTIVE BRIGHT is therefore regarded as a skin restoration innovation that is effective and safe for every skin type, bringing the skin back to its former beauty. After ACTIVE BRIGHT, there may be slight scabbing at spots with strong color, such as freckles, acne marks or dark spots. The scabs then fall off on their own and the dark spots fade. Avoid strong sunlight for about 1-2 weeks and apply sunscreen regularly. Treatment depends on the diagnosis of the dermatologist at Doctor Younger Clinic

When Results Start to Show

You can usually see changes in your skin from the first treatment, but results become clearly visible after 3-5 continuous sessions (depending on each person's skin condition). Each treatment takes only 15-30 minutes
