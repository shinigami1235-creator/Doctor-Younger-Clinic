How AURA BRIGHT Works

AURA BRIGHT is a laser that helps restore and nourish the skin and solve skin problems. It fades melasma, freckles, red and dark marks from acne, capillaries on the face, and various scars, making the face fairer and brighter. It uses FAST EDGE MICRO PULSE technology, which combines two types of laser particles with different wavelengths: a yellow laser with a wavelength of 578 nm. and a green laser with a wavelength of 511 nm. This makes it effective for a variety of skin problems such as melasma, freckles, and red and dark marks from acne, so the skin looks fairer and brighter, without damaging the outer layer of the skin. The skin feels comfortable, or there is very little pain, during treatment. Melasma, freckles and various scars look fainter and the face looks fairer and brighter. Suitable for all skin tones.
