Scars, pitted skin from acne scars, and wrinkles are major problems for many people. Everyone likes a face that is bright, firm and free of wrinkles. We recommend a laser that helps tighten pores and can treat various acne scars.

Results from FRACTIONAL CO2 LASER

  • Helps care for acne scars and stretch marks
  • Helps stimulate the production of new collagen in the skin
  • Helps skin become firm and taut
  • Helps tighten pores and smooth the skin's condition
  • Helps fade various wrinkles, making the face brighter

How FRACTIONAL CO2 LASER Works

FRACTIONAL CO2 LASER fires the laser as many tiny dots onto the skin in a PIXEL pattern, with many dots per square inch, which is why it is also called PIXEL LASER. It is a process that stimulates skin cell renewal, which Doctor Younger Clinic uses to restore and nourish the skin of people with wrinkles, pitted skin and scars, to effectively improve the facial skin and stimulate new collagen production.

FRACTIONAL CO2 LASER has properties suited to people with scars, pitted skin, skin that is not firm, wrinkled and creased skin, and acne scars. It also helps treat surgical scars and stretch marks, renews skin cells and tightens pores. This laser can be used on the face and body. Treatment depends on the diagnosis of the dermatologist at Doctor Younger Clinic

FRACTIONAL CO2 LASER Is Suitable for Treating

  • People with scars and acne scars
  • Wide pores and skin lacking firmness
  • Stretch marks
  • People with sagging skin and wrinkles
  • Making the skin look fair, bright and clear
  • Raised scars and scars from surgery
