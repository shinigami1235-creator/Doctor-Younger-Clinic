Totally fed up with the same old hair removal methods, whether plucking, shaving, waxing or using hair removal cream, that you have to repeat again and again and that leave ingrown hairs and chicken skin behind!!! Stop worrying about hair with permanent hair removal laser TKS LASER from DOCTOR YOUNGER. Hair becomes noticeably thinner even after the first treatment, pores are tightened, skin is smooth, fair and bright, sweating and body odor are reduced, and there is no more bothersome hair.

Results of permanent hair removal with TKS LASER

  • Hair in the treated area is reduced by about 20-30% from the first treatment (depending on each person's condition)
  • The treated area will have smooth skin
  • Pores become tighter
  • Helps tighten pores, with skin that is smooth, fair and bright, reduces sweating and body odor, and leaves no bothersome hair
  • Helps reduce sweating, so body odor lessens

The APOGEE machine is a permanent hair removal technology that has passed clinical trials at a medical institution and is proven to be a very effective permanent hair removal laser. It helps solve ingrown hairs and strong body odor caused by heavy hair growth, and helps tighten pores, giving smooth, fair, bright skin. The number of permanent hair removal sessions depends on the doctor's diagnosis.

There may be many hair removal methods, but the results differ depending on the equipment and how it works. Body odor and ingrown hair problems are clearly solved by TKS LASER, a permanent hair removal method that works precisely. It uses the light absorption of the pigment in the hair shaft to carry the heat of the laser deep down to destroy the parts that make hair grow back naturally, the BULGE and the BULB. Once these 2 key parts are destroyed, the hair can no longer grow back.

Steps of permanent hair removal with TKS LASER

  • Before seeing the doctor at Doctor Younger Clinic or having laser treatment, clients must not shave, pluck, wax or use hair removal products for 1-2 weeks beforehand.
  • The doctor examines the size of the hair and diagnoses the treatment.
  • The doctor's assistant prepares the client by cleaning the skin in the area to be treated. If the hair is too long, the assistant shaves the area a little more so it is suitable for receiving the laser beam, for the best effect in destroying the hair.
  • Before the laser, the doctor's assistant applies cooling gel to the area to be treated and fits eye protection to keep the laser light out of the client's eyes.
  • The doctor adjusts the laser power to suit each person's skin color, for good and safe results.
  • During the treatment, the doctor's assistant blows cool air and applies cooling gel to relieve the heat of the laser.
  • When the treatment is finished, the doctor's assistant cleans the area and applies anti-inflammatory medication.
