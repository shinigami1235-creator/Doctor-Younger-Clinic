Results of VITAL LIFT

  • Facial skin is lifted and firm
  • Helps fade various wrinkles
  • Helps stimulate collagen and elastin production
  • Nourishes skin so it is smooth, soft, moisturized and full of life

VITAL LIFT is a technology that helps fill the facial skin, lift and firm it, fade wrinkles and nourish the skin. It works through a vacuum suction device together with AUTO MESO THERAPY, a technology that combines a skin-lifting technique with vitamins and hyaluronic acid, delivered evenly into the skin, so the facial skin is lifted. You should have VITAL LIFT treatment regularly every 3-6 months at first (depending on each person's skin condition) for effective results. After treatment there is only slight redness, which goes away on its own within 3-4 days.
