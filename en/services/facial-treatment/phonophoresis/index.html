Results of PHONOPHORESIS TREATMENT

  • Helps nourish skin so it is moisturized and softer
  • Helps reduce dullness of the facial skin and the skin under the eyes
  • Helps fade dark marks for fair, bright skin
  • Helps lighten melasma and freckles for a younger look

How PHONOPHORESIS TREATMENT works

A treatment that uses high-frequency sound waves as a medium to push Doctor Younger Clinic's vitamins into the skin. Pushing vitamins in this way lets them sink deeper into the skin than applying them on the surface alone, and with the benefits of the vitamins, skin becomes moisturized, soft and smooth. It also helps fade melasma, freckles, dark marks and dullness across the face and under the eyes, making the skin fairer and brighter.
