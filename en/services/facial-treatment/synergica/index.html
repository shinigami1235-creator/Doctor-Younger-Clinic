Results of SYNERGICA TREATMENT

  • Helps stimulate blood circulation for smooth, soft skin
  • Helps skin look taut and firm, restoring a youthful look to the face
  • Helps skin look smooth, soft and moisturized
  • Helps skin look fair and bright
  • Stimulates collagen production

How SYNERGICA TREATMENT works

A treatment that combines an electrical charge technique with amber light to push vitamins deep into the skin cells and help stimulate new collagen production. Together with a facial massage technique that stimulates blood circulation so it can nourish the skin evenly, it helps skin look moisturized, taut, fairer and brighter, so the complexion looks even more youthful.
