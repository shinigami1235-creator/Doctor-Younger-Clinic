How ENDERMOLOGIE® Works

ENDERMOLOGIE® is an innovation imported from France for solving all kinds of fat problems. It is a program for reducing cellulite and eliminating excess fat with the CELLU M6® KEY MODULE machine, without surgery or any medication. The program uses a 3-direction massage technique (massage and suction) to rearrange the connective tissue of the skin into a more orderly and firmer structure. While you are being massaged, fat is also burned, and blood and lymph circulation is stimulated, helping the body remove more waste. Skin cells receive more nourishment, which leads to increased production of collagen and elastin, so the skin becomes smooth and firm and the figure becomes slimmer, because of the reduction in cellulite and excess fat. In addition, during treatment with the KEY MODULE program you will feel relaxed and comfortable, as a result of the muscles relaxing during the massage.
